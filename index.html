About Us

Golden Ring Donuts opened its doors in 2019 as a tiny bakery-café tucked into San Antonio's Pearl District area. We believe a donut should taste like Sunday morning — warm, buttery, and made with care.

Every morning our bakers knead, proof, and fry fresh donuts in small batches, glazing them just before the doors open. No freezer dough, no shortcuts — just honest, golden-ring goodness.
